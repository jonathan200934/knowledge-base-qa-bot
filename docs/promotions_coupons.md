# Promotions and Coupons Policy

## Coupon Eligibility

Coupon codes may be limited by customer segment, region, product category, minimum order amount, or promotion period.

## Stacking Rules

Only one coupon code can be used per order unless the promotion explicitly allows stacking. Store credit can be combined with most coupons.

## Price Adjustments

We do not retroactively apply coupon codes after checkout. Price adjustments may be offered only for approved promotional errors.

## Abuse Prevention

Orders may be cancelled if coupon abuse, duplicate account creation, or resale behavior is detected.
