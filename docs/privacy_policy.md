# Privacy Policy

## Data We Collect

We collect account details, order information, shipping address, payment status, support messages, and device metadata needed to operate the store.

## Data Use

Customer data is used to process orders, prevent fraud, provide support, personalize recommendations, and satisfy legal accounting obligations.

## Marketing Preferences

Customers can unsubscribe from promotional email at any time. Transactional emails such as receipts and shipping notices cannot be disabled.

## Data Deletion

Customers can request deletion from Privacy Settings. Some order records may be retained when required for tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
