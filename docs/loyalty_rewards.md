# Loyalty Rewards FAQ

## Earning Points

Members earn points on eligible purchases after the order ships. Taxes, shipping fees, gift cards, and refunded items do not earn points.

## Redeeming Points

Points can be redeemed for discount codes during checkout. Points cannot be exchanged for cash or applied to past orders.

## Tier Benefits

Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers unlock benefits such as early sale access, birthday coupons, and higher points multipliers.

## Point Expiration

Unused points expire after 12 months of account inactivity. Customers receive an email reminder before points expire.
