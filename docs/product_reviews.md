# Product Reviews Policy

## Review Eligibility

Customers can review products they purchased after the order is delivered. Review invitations may be sent by email.

## Moderation Rules

Reviews may be removed if they include hate speech, private information, spam, unrelated complaints, or false claims about competitors.

## Incentivized Reviews

Incentivized reviews must be clearly disclosed. Free product samples do not guarantee positive review placement.

## Review Updates

Customers can update a review if their experience changes after support, warranty service, or extended product use.
