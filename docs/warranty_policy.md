# Warranty Policy

## Warranty Coverage

Eligible products include a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects under normal use. Accessories and consumables may have shorter coverage.

## Warranty Claim Requirements

Customers must provide the order number, product serial number if available, photos or video of the defect, and a clear description of the issue.

## Warranty Exclusions

Warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized repair, normal wear, cosmetic scratches, or damage caused by incompatible accessories.

## Repair or Replacement

Approved warranty claims may result in repair, replacement, store credit, or refund depending on inventory and local consumer law.
