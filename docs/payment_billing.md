# Payment and Billing FAQ

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, selected mobile wallets, and store credit. Available payment methods can vary by country and currency.

## Failed Payment

If payment fails, customers should verify card details, billing address, available funds, and bank authorization. Unpaid orders are not reserved.

## Invoice Requests

Customers can download receipts from Order History. Business invoices require the legal company name, tax ID, and billing email before shipment.

## Currency Conversion

International orders are charged in the checkout currency. The customer's bank may apply its own exchange rate or foreign transaction fee.
