# International Shipping FAQ

## Supported Countries

International shipping is available to selected countries shown at checkout. Some products cannot ship internationally due to carrier or regulatory limits.

## Customs and Duties

Customers are responsible for import duties, taxes, brokerage fees, and customs delays unless checkout explicitly says duties are prepaid.

## Delivery Estimate

International delivery usually takes 7-14 business days after shipment, but customs inspection can extend the timeline.

## Restricted Items

Batteries, liquids, oversized goods, and personalized products may be restricted for some international destinations.
